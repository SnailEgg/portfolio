Video Game Development

Smile for the Camera

2026

An experimental tech demo that uses the webcam on your computer to let you control the game with your facial expressions! The game made in Unreal Engine communicates with a locally-running Python process which uses OpenCV and ResMaskNet to detect the emotion on the player's face in real time.

One Small Breath

2025

A Next.js app which explores using LLMs to deliver a human-written narrative, in One Small Breath you must talk to an AI chatbot to learn how to escape the room you wake up in.

Lagum

2025

In Lagum you can slow the framerate for everything except yourself. Made in Unity with a friend in three days for the Patch Notes v1.0 game jam.
